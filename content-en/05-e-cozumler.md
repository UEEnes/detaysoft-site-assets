[P] What needs do Financial Solutions cover?
[S] Financial Solutions cover e-Account Statement, e-Reconciliation, e-Payment, Online DBS, DAS, Inflation Accounting, Z+Pack and e-Tax Return. Each solution focuses on a different area of finance operations.
[P] What is the difference between e-Account Statement and e-Reconciliation?
[S] e-Account Statement focuses on tracking account and transaction information from different banks. e-Reconciliation, on the other hand, enables current account and reconciliation processes to be carried out electronically and responses to be tracked.

[P] What areas do Inflation Accounting, Z+Pack and e-Tax Return support?
[S] Inflation Accounting focuses on handling financial statements according to the effects of inflation, Z+Pack on expense and budget planning, and e-Tax Return on tax return preparation and checking the related financial data.
