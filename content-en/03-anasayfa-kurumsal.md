[P] What is United VARs?
[S] United VARs is an international community of partners that brings together SAP solution partners in more than 100 countries. The network includes more than 80 SAP Platinum partners and over 20,000 consultants.
[P] What is Detaysoft's role within United VARs?
[S] Detaysoft is the only United VARs member in Türkiye and serves on the community's Steering Committee.
[P] How does the United VARs network contribute to international SAP projects?
[S] The network enables local SAP expertise in different countries to work together under shared project standards. As a result, global implementation, roll-out, and localization processes can be coordinated across different geographies.

[S] Projects draw on local teams who know the language, culture, and legal requirements of the country concerned. In this way, the global project structure is addressed together with local needs.

[S] Depending on project needs, 24/7 technical support can be provided across different time zones and languages
